# 1048:156a inferred_parse_mirror_section
Status: understood · Cluster: script and rooms · Convention: cdecl far, caller cleans

**Purpose (confirmed):** Read a room file's mirror section (`MirrorStart=` to `MirrorEnd=`): where and how the player's reflection is drawn.
**Inputs:** The room record (far).
**Outputs / state written:** Entries are 0 to 24 and each entry keeps its data in parallel arrays of the room record. **`Cond=`** (1) starts the next entry: advances the entry index and parses condition terms into a new list node whose far pointer goes to `+0x11bf + 4 * entry`; **`Cond2=`** (0x15) chains an alternative list. **`PosX=`** (0x16) takes three long numbers into `+0x131d`, `+0x1381` and `+0x13e5` (plus 4 per entry): the coefficients A, B, C of the reflected x = (x * A + y * B + C) / 256. **`PosY=`** (0x17) takes three into `+0x1449`, `+0x14ad` and `+0x1511`: D, E, F for y. **`ScaleVal=`** (0x18) takes three numbers into words at `+0x12eb`, `+0x1575` and `+0x15a7` (plus 2 per entry): the first is the mirror's facing used by `1038:01e4`; the other two are not used. **`ClipXY=`** (0x3e) takes four numbers (left, top, right, bottom) into words at `+0x1223`, `+0x1255`, `+0x1287`, `+0x12b9` (plus 2 per entry): the clip rectangle of the reflection.
