# 1038:01e4 inferred_update_room_sprites
Status: understood · Cluster: graphics and bitmaps · Convention: cdecl far, caller cleans

**Purpose (confirmed in outline):** Redraw the whole room at once: show every room sprite at its current frame, then the player and the player's mirror image, then repaint the screen. Used after a room change and after a cut-scene wait ends.
**Inputs:** A device context (the window's).
**Outputs / state written:** For every sprite node of the room (list at room +0x39, next at +0x681), if its conditions are true (`1060:07f6` on +0xd): takes the image of the current frame (+0x73, table at +0x77); when the node is flagged for scaling (+0x67 bit 4) it computes a scale (the frame's own value at +0x57b, or `(frame y - far line) * 128 / range`, clamped to 10 to 180, from the room's `ScaleFactor=` words at +0x33/+0x35), frees the old scaled pixels (+0x277), scales the picture into the node's scaled record with `1020:0656`, marks it keyed and uses it as the image; puts the image into the node's sprite record (+0x67d) with the frame's x and y (+0x37b, +0x47b) and calls `1078:18d8` (update sprite) with the node's list number (+0x6d). A node flagged with bit 3 ends the scan. A node whose conditions are false and whose sprite index is 100 (own record) is reset to frame 0 with no image and updated. **Player:** if `EraseMike` is non-zero both player sprite records (far pointers at `10e0:0030` and `10e0:0034`) get no image and are updated. Otherwise, if `ShowMike` is non-zero: frees the buffer `DS:5cc4`; if the room's mirror condition (+0x11bf) holds, the mirror sprite gets an image scaled from the player's picture set `DS:8776` (216 bytes per direction) for the direction `(room mirror direction at +0x12eb - facing) & 7`, flipped when that is 1 to 3, positioned by an affine map of the player's position (x' = (x*A + y*B + C) >> 8 and y' likewise, coefficients at +0x131d..+0x1511 of the room record) and clipped to the room's mirror rectangle (+0x1223..+0x12b9); otherwise the mirror sprite is hidden. Then the player itself: scale `(y - far line) * 134 / range`, clamped 10 to 180 (flip when the facing is 1 to 3), image from set `DS:8776 + facing * 216`, at the player's position (`DS:3cae`, `DS:3cb0`), on sprite list 0. Finally resets the dirty rectangle to the full 640 by 480 screen and composes the scene over the room backdrop (`1078:1796`).
**Calls:** `1060:07f6`, `1060:0b00` (variables `EraseMike`, `ShowMike`), `1020:0656` (scale a picture), `1078:18d8` (update a sprite), `1078:1030`, `1078:1796` (compose), `1090:0068`, 32-bit multiply and divide helpers.
**Yields / blocks / timing:** none
**Edge cases:** The decompiler wrongly reads many of the room-record offsets as string labels (for example `s_Dark_Seed_II` is the far pointer at `10e0:0030`). The reading above is from the disassembly. The player's frame is always the standing picture here (walking frames are chosen in `1038:1ade`).
