**Purpose (inferred):** One step of a pixel-by-pixel dissolve between two pictures, driven by a shift-register sequence.
**Inputs:** Two image records (far).
**Outputs / state written:** On the first call it works out the pixel count and the register's polynomial from the bit length (`1020:0a14`) and clears pixel 0 of the first picture and sets pixel 0 of the second to 255. After 32 calls the register restarts.
