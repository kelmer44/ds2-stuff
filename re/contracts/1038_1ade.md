# 1038:1ade inferred_world_step
Status: understood · Cluster: graphics and bitmaps · Convention: cdecl far, caller cleans

**Purpose (confirmed in outline):** One step of the world: animate the room's sprites, move the player along the walk path, and redraw the changed sprites. The game tick calls it when a room has just been entered, and after loading a saved game.
**Inputs:** The window.
**Outputs / state written:** Sets the cursor from the cursor mode, marks itself busy (`DS:16b2`) and takes a random number. **Sprites:** for each node of the room's sprite list, if its conditions are false it resets (frame 0) and updates; if true and the random number is inside the node's window (+0x69 < r < +0x6b, always true right after a room change) it plays the effect or speech sound when the frame reaches +0x45 or +0x54 (`10a8:06fa`), takes the frame's picture, scales it when flagged (as `1038:01e4`), or for nodes flagged "follows the player" (bit 3) records the player's position as that frame's anchor and builds a scaled copy for the player's scale; counts steps on the frame (+0x75) and advances the frame when more than the hold count (+0x71); at the end frame (+0x65) it loops to the start frame (+0x63) if flagged (bit 2) or stays, and applies the `Change=` list (`1060:092e`); updates the sprite when the image, position or flags changed or a room has just been entered. **Player:** a `ShowMike` value of 2 is set to 1. The step length is `|y - far line| * 14 / range` clamped to 6 to 14. If the player is within one step (both axes) of the next path point and points remain, the next point is taken (cell centre: x = (cell mod columns) * 10 - 75, y = row * H + H/2 + Y0 from the room's walk-map numbers) and the path index goes down. If farther than a step: the walking frame `DS:16b0` goes 1 to 8 and back to 1, the direction angle comes from `10a0:00be`, and the position moves by one step along it using the sine and cosine tables (`10a0:005e`, `10a0:0000`, `10a0:0080`). The facing `DS:8e3c` (0 to 7: 0 up, 1 up-right, 2 right, 3 down-right, 4 down, 5 down-left, 6 left, 7 up-left) is chosen from the signs of the difference: if |dx| > 5 |dy| it is left or right, else up or down, unless 2 |dy| < 5 |dx| when it is the diagonal. In room 0x25b a facing of 7 stays 7. If there is no path left and the player has not yet been put at the target (`DS:0086`/`0088`), or a target facing (`DS:6112`, below 8) is pending, the player is placed at the target, takes that facing, the walk frame resets and the pending facing becomes 8. If the player moved, the player's and mirror's pictures are rebuilt exactly as in `1038:01e4` but with the picture `facing * 9 + walk frame` of the set (the walk frames), the sprites are updated, and the scene is composed (`1078:1796`) with the dirty rectangle reset to the full screen. Clears the room-changed flag `DS:610e` and the busy flag.
