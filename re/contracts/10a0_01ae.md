# 10a0:01ae inferred_vector_length
Status: understood · Cluster: startup helpers · Convention: cdecl far, caller cleans

**Purpose (inferred):** Length of a vector (dx, dy) computed from the larger component and the angle.
**Inputs:** Two signed words: dx and dy.
**Outputs / state written:** Gets the angle with `10a0:00be` (0 to 255, wrapped to that range). For angles up to 128 it divides a quarter of |dy| (rounded toward zero for odd values) by the cosine of the angle (`10a0:005e`); otherwise it divides a quarter of |dx| by the sine (`10a0:0000`). Returns the quotient.
**Calls:** `10a0:00be`, `10a0:005e`, `10a0:0000`.
**Yields / blocks / timing:** none
**Edge cases:** The sine and cosine come from a table; a zero value would divide by zero. The scale of the table (a factor of 4 in this formula) is from the code, not tested.
**Evidence:** `re/export/fn/10a0_01ae.c`; used by the walking code (`1038:086e`, `1038:1ade`).
