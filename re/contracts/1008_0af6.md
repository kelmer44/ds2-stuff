# 1008:0af6 inferred_open_inventory_bar
Status: understood · Cluster: main window and input · Convention: cdecl far, caller cleans

**Purpose (confirmed in outline):** Set up the inventory bar when the inventory opens: its bitmap, its pictures, the dimmed background and the list of items the player has.
**Inputs:** none
**Outputs / state written:** Makes a 0x428-byte copy of the picture header template at `DS:1fce` and sets it to 640 wide and 70 high (the sign of the height follows the template), creates the bar's WinG bitmap and loads five pictures from the game files: frame `invntry1`, `arwlft2`, `arwlft1`, `arwrght2` and `arwrght1`, each with its palette. Builds the dim table with `1008:0f86`. Copies the bottom 70 rows of the room picture (from row 410) into the bar, keeps a copy of those 640 by 70 pixels in a block at `DS:2d62` and passes pixels 60 to 580 of rows 10 to 59 of that copy through the dim table. Then clears the item count, the pixel width `DS:0d24` and the scroll position, and walks the global list of item records (head `DS:8728`; each record has a sub-list at +0x10 whose records chain at +0x93): for every record whose condition list is true (`1060:07f6`), it stores the record pointers in the arrays at `DS:8e3e` and `DS:94ca`, loads the picture named in the record (+0x2b) into a 24-byte slot, adds 50 to `DS:0d24` and 1 to `DS:0d26`.
**Calls:** `1090:0000` (alloc), `HMemCpy`, `WinGCreateBitmap`, `1080:0890` (load image), `1080:0ad6` (palette), `1008:0f86`, `1080:0d56` (draw image), `1060:07f6` (test conditions).
**Yields / blocks / timing:** none
**Edge cases:** Stops scanning a sub-list at the first item that passes (the loop exits when a flag is set after the first match in the sub-list); the exact exit rule of the inner loop is only read from the decompiler output. No limit on the number of items is checked here, the arrays have room for the whole item set.
