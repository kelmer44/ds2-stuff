# 1068:1efe inferred_parse_conv_condition
Status: understood · Cluster: conversations · Convention: cdecl far, caller cleans

**Purpose (confirmed):** Parse the text of a `hide`, `unhide`, `goto`, `destroy` or `assign` line into a 0x46-byte record (confirmed).
**Inputs:** A far pointer to the new record, the value text of the line and the line type (7 hide, 8 unhide, 10 goto, 11 assign, 12 destroy).
**Outputs / state written:** If the text starts with `(` the balanced parenthesised part is compiled (`1068:1e12`) into the record's condition (cells at +2, the length at +0); without it the condition is the constant 1 (always true). For hide, unhide, goto and destroy the rest of the text is copied to the target-name field (+0x2e, at most 0x14 characters). For assign the rest must be `Variable,expression`: it is split at the comma (error "Expression without ','" when there is none), a 0x28-byte assignment record is allocated (variable index in its first word, compiled expression after it) and linked at +0x2a/+0x2c.
**Calls:** `1068:1e12`, `1060:02bc`, `1090:0000`, `lstrcpyn`, `1098:0248`.
**Yields / blocks / timing:** none
**Edge cases:** The name field takes 20 characters at most: `exit,Greeting` is kept whole (the comma stays), so it never matches a node name.
**Evidence:** `re/export/fn/1068_1efe.c`; the forms `assign = speaker, 0` and `assign = TalkedMom, 1` in the real files.
